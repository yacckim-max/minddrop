Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MindDrop ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the MindDrop application and website (collectively, the "Service"). By using MindDrop, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Summary: MindDrop collects only the minimum data necessary to operate. We do not sell your data. We do not store your TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram credentials on our servers.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you authorize MindDrop to connect to your social media accounts, we receive and temporarily store:

  • OAuth access tokens — temporary authentication tokens granted by TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram via their official OAuth 2.0 flows. These are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to MindDrop servers.
  • Account identifiers — platform-specific user IDs (e.g., TikTok open_id) required to publish content on your behalf.
  • Contact information — if you contact us via email, we retain your email address and correspondence for support purposes.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Service, we may automatically collect:

  • Usage data — information about how the application is used, including actions performed and errors encountered, stored locally for debugging purposes.
  • Video metadata — titles, descriptions, and hashtags of published videos, stored locally in the application's database.
  • Analytics data — view counts, engagement metrics, and other performance data retrieved from platform APIs (YouTube Analytics, TikTok API). This data is stored locally and is never transmitted to third parties.

1.3 Information from Third-Party Platforms

When you connect your social media accounts, we access only the data required by the scopes you authorize:

  • TikTok: user.info.basic (display name, avatar), video.publish (upload videos), video.upload (initiate uploads)
  • YouTube: youtube.upload (upload videos and manage your YouTube content)
  • Instagram: Basic account information and media publishing permissions (when Instagram integration is enabled)

We do not access your followers, messages, contacts, or any data beyond what is required for content publishing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Authenticate with third-party platforms on your behalf
  • Publish video content to your authorized social media accounts
  • Retrieve performance analytics for your published content
  • Improve the reliability and performance of the Service
  • Respond to your support requests

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the Service.

3. Data Storage and Security

MindDrop is designed as a self-hosted, locally-run application. All data — including OAuth tokens, video metadata, and analytics — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate centralized servers that store your personal data.

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including:

  • OAuth tokens are stored in local configuration files with restricted access
  • No personal data is transmitted over unencrypted connections
  • API credentials are stored in environment variable files excluded from version control

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your consent: We share data with third-party platforms (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram) only as authorized by you through the OAuth flow.
  • Service providers: We use the following third-party APIs to operate the Service: OpenAI (text and audio generation), Pexels (stock footage), and cloud storage providers if configured. Each provider's own privacy policy governs their data practices.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal process.

5. Third-Party Services

MindDrop uses the following third-party services. Please review their privacy policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy
  • Instagram / Meta Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Pexels Privacy Policy

6. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained locally until you revoke access via the relevant platform or manually delete your local configuration. Video metadata and analytics are retained locally for as long as you use the Service. You may delete all local data at any time by removing the application and its configuration files.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, or to revoke MindDrop's access to your social media accounts, please contact us at yacckim@gmail.com or manage permissions directly in your TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram account settings.

8. Children's Privacy

MindDrop is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

9. International Data Transfers

MindDrop operates primarily as a locally-run application. If you use third-party APIs (TikTok, OpenAI, etc.), data may be transmitted to and processed in countries other than your own, subject to those providers' data protection measures.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Service after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
