How it works

Nothing is ever posted without your review and explicit confirmation.

1

Connect your TikTok account

Click “Continue with TikTok”, log in on TikTok and approve the permissions MindDrop asks for. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok settings.

2

MindDrop generates your video

MindDrop writes, voices and animates a short “what if” science episode of The Lab, with captions, and prepares a suggested title and hashtags.

3

You review it and post

You preview the video, edit the title, choose who can view it and your interaction settings, then post it via the TikTok Content Posting API.

  • Posting to: your TikTok nickname is shown
  • Privacy: you choose (no default)
  • Comments / Duet / Stitch: off until you enable them
  • Commercial content disclosure when relevant

See the publishing screen →

What MindDrop asks TikTok for

  • user.info.basic — your open_id, display name and avatar, to show which account you are posting to.
  • video.upload & video.publish — to send the video you approved to your TikTok account (Content Posting API).

MindDrop never sees your TikTok password, does not read your followers, messages or other videos, and never posts without your confirmation. Details in our Privacy Policy.
