Review & post to TikTok

After you connect your TikTok account, every video MindDrop generates goes through this review screen. You decide what is posted, with which caption and privacy setting. Nothing is uploaded to TikTok until you press Post to TikTok.

Illustration only. This page shows the layout of the MindDrop publishing screen; the controls below are disabled and nothing on this page connects to TikTok or posts content. In the live screen, the options come from your TikTok account (creator info) at the moment you open it.

Off by default. When on, choose “Your brand” and/or “Branded content”; the matching label (“Promotional content” / “Paid partnership”) is shown and branded content cannot be private.

By posting, you agree to TikTok’s Music Usage Confirmation.

After posting: “Your video is being processed by TikTok. It may take a few minutes to appear on your profile.”

  • Data used on this screen: your TikTok display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic) and the video you approved (scopes video.upload, video.publish).
  • MindDrop adds no watermark or promotional branding to your video.
  • You can disconnect MindDrop at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions.
